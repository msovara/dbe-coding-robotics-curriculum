# Workshop Quality Feedback Session

**Audience:** Facilitators (closing session, Day 5)  
**Time:** 15–20 minutes (or 10 minutes with the short set)

**Download slides:** [Workshop_Quality_Feedback_Session.pptx](Workshop_Quality_Feedback_Session.pptx)

Use the written form for detail: [Workshop evaluation form](workshop-evaluation-form.md).

---

## Purpose

Get honest feedback on the **quality of the workshop** so the next cohort is better. Names optional.

---

## How to run it

1. **Frame (1 min)** — honest feedback; names optional.
2. **Silent write (5 min)** — sticky notes or evaluation form.
3. **Share-out (8–10 min)** — each table: **1 keep** + **1 improve**.
4. **Close (2 min)** — thank them; note what you will act on.

**If you only have 10 minutes:** ask questions **1, 2, 3, 8, and 10**.

---

## Questions (workshop quality)

1. Overall, how would you rate this workshop? (1–5)
2. What was the **strongest** part of the week?
3. What was the **weakest** part, and how would you fix it?
4. Was the pace too fast / just right / too slow?
5. Did theory and hands-on feel balanced?
6. How clear and helpful were the facilitators?
7. How useful were the materials (guides, `.sb3` files, slides)?
8. How confident do you feel to train or teach this after the workshop? (1–5)
9. Would you recommend this workshop to a colleague? Why / why not?
10. **One change** that would most improve quality next time?

---

## Facilitator tips

- Ask for **examples**, not slogans (“Day 2 Catch Game demo was clear”).
- Separate **content** vs **delivery** vs **logistics**.
- Don’t defend in the room — note it, thank them, move on.

**Exit ticket:** *This workshop would be excellent if…*
