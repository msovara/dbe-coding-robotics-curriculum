Dear [Name/Colleagues],

I am pleased to invite you to a **5-day Coding and Robotics Teacher Training Workshop** designed specifically for DBE educators.

**Workshop Content:**
The program provides comprehensive training in:
• Scratch programming and computational thinking
• Arduino physical computing with Scratch for Arduino
• Coding & Robotics with Arduino and Micro:bit (Days 3–4)
• Code Club introduction and action planning (Day 5)
• Curriculum integration and assessment strategies

**Expected Outcomes:**
Participants will gain the skills to teach coding and robotics in their classrooms and successfully integrate these technologies across DBE curriculum subjects.

**Practical Details:**
- **Duration:** 5 consecutive days
- **Daily Schedule:** 09:00 - 17:00
- **Format:** Hands-on, project-based learning
- **Resources:** All necessary hardware and software will be provided

[Insert specific details: venue, dates, registration deadline, contact person, etc.]

Please confirm your attendance by [date]. For any queries, please contact [contact details].

We look forward to welcoming you to this training opportunity.

Yours sincerely,

[Your Name]  
[Your Position]  
[Organization]  
[Contact Details]
