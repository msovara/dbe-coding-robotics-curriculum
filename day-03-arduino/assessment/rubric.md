# Day 3 Assessment Rubric
## Coding & Robotics with Arduino

## Overall Assessment Framework

### Formative Assessment (Throughout Day)
- Observation during multi-sensor and robotics activities
- Quick check-ins on variables, logic, and sequencing
- Wiring and code verification
- Participation in project planning and sharing

### Summative Assessment (End of Day)
- Completed Coding & Robotics project (Arduino)
- Use of at least one sensor and one actuator (or multiple sensors)
- Brief explanation of behaviour and logic
- Daily reflection and self-assessment

---

## Rubric 1: Extended Arduino / S4A Skills

| Component | Not Yet (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Multi-sensor use** | Uses only one input | Uses two inputs with support | Combines two or more inputs correctly | Designs logic that integrates multiple inputs creatively |
| **Variables** | Does not use variables | Uses variables with guidance | Uses variables for thresholds, state, or counters | Uses variables effectively for calibration and logic |
| **Conditions** | No or incorrect conditions | Simple if-then | If-then-else and comparisons | Nested or combined conditions for complex behaviour |
| **Sequences / timing** | No use of timing | Uses wait blocks | Creates clear sequences with loops and wait | Designs timed behaviours (e.g. traffic light, step-by-step) |
| **Actuator control** | Cannot control servo/motor | Controls with help | Controls servo or motor correctly | Integrates actuators with sensor logic |

**Evidence:** S4A scripts, circuit in use, facilitator observation.

---

## Rubric 2: Robotics / Behaviour Design

| Component | Not Yet (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Defining behaviour** | Cannot describe what the system should do | Describes in vague terms | Describes clear input–output behaviour | Describes and refines behaviour with thresholds/options |
| **Planning (inputs/outputs)** | No planning | Lists some components | Lists inputs, outputs, and pins | Plans with diagram or clear pin list and logic |
| **Implementing behaviour** | Project does not match plan | Partial match | Project matches planned behaviour | Exceeds plan with improvements or extensions |
| **Iteration** | Does not test or adjust | Tests with facilitator help | Tests and makes at least one improvement | Tests, debugs, and improves independently |

**Evidence:** Project plan (written or verbal), final project demo, reflection.

---

## Rubric 3: Project Completion and Presentation

| Aspect | Not Yet (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|--------|-------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Functionality** | Project does not run or behave as intended | Works partially or with errors | Works as intended | Works reliably and with optional extensions |
| **Wiring and safety** | Unsafe or incorrect wiring | Wiring mostly correct with help | Correct and safe wiring | Neat, correct, and clearly labelled |
| **Explanation** | Cannot explain how it works | Explains with significant support | Explains logic and components clearly | Explains and links to classroom or real-world use |
| **Collaboration** | Does not share or help others | Shares when asked | Shares project and helps peers | Actively supports others and gives useful feedback |

**Evidence:** Demo, short showcase, peer feedback, facilitator notes.

---

## Rubric 4: Classroom and Curriculum Readiness

| Component | Not Yet (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Curriculum link** | Cannot identify link | Identifies one subject or topic | Links to DBE Technology/coding or other subject | Plans a concrete lesson or activity using this project |
| **Differentiation** | No ideas | One idea for different learners | Several ideas (simplify/extend) | Clear plan for different levels and group sizes |
| **Resource use** | Unclear what is needed | Lists some components | Lists components and time needed | Plans for kit reuse, storage, and sharing in class |
| **Troubleshooting** | Relies entirely on facilitator | Uses troubleshooting guide with help | Uses guide and tries fixes independently | Can anticipate common issues and prevent them |

**Evidence:** Reflection, action ideas, optional lesson or activity note.

---

## How to Use This Rubric

- **During the day:** Use Rubrics 1 and 2 to observe progress in activities and project development.
- **End of day:** Use Rubrics 3 and 4 for project showcase and reflection.
- **Scale:** 1 = Not yet, 2 = Developing, 3 = Proficient, 4 = Advanced. An overall “Proficient” for the day might be: most criteria at 2–3, with at least one project that works and is explained.
- **Feedback:** Share one strength and one next step with each participant where possible.

## Notes for Facilitators

- Day 3 builds on Day 2; if someone struggled with S4A or wiring, allow more time on review and pair work.
- Emphasise “behaviour” and “planning” as much as wiring and code.
