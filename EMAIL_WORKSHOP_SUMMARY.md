## DBE Coding and Robotics Teacher Training Workshop

---

## Short Version (For Email)

**DBE Coding and Robotics Teacher Training Workshop**

A comprehensive 5-day workshop designed to equip teachers with coding and robotics skills for classroom implementation.

**Topics Covered:**
- **Day 1:** Scratch programming fundamentals and computational thinking
- **Day 2:** Physical computing with Arduino and Scratch for Arduino (S4A)
- **Day 3:** Coding & Robotics with Arduino (extended projects, sensors, robotics)
- **Day 4:** Coding & Robotics with Micro:bit – Part 1 (MakeCode, inputs/outputs)
- **Day 5:** Coding & Robotics with Micro:bit – Part 2 (Code Club Intro, wrap-up, action planning)

**Outcomes:** Teachers will be able to teach coding/robotics, integrate into DBE subjects, and run similar workshops in their schools.

**Duration:** 5 days (09:00-17:00 daily)  
**Format:** Hands-on, project-based learning  
**Resources:** All materials and hardware provided
